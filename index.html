// Learning Journey

6 Months of
Deep Immersion

Our curriculum is structured as a progressive deep-dive — from Python fundamentals all the way to training and deploying large language models and multimodal AI systems.

Month 1–2
Python, NumPy, Pandas, Data Wrangling & Exploratory Analysis
Month 3
Classical ML: Regression, Classification, Clustering, Ensemble Methods
Month 4
Deep Learning: CNNs, RNNs, Transformers, Attention Mechanisms
Month 5–6
LLMs, RAG Systems, Deployment, MLOps & Capstone Projects
